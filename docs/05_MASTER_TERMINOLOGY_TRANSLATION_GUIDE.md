# Mall Electro Master Terminology Translation Guide

Last updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

This guide establishes preferred translation direction for Mall Electro across Bulgarian, English and Romanian. It should be used before any multilingual publishing work begins.

The goal is consistency, technical clarity and B2B industrial accuracy.

## Translation Rules

- Translate for industrial B2B clarity, not consumer marketing tone.
- Prefer practical engineering language over promotional language.
- Keep service names stable across the site.
- Keep industry names stable across the site.
- Do not alternate between multiple English or Romanian labels for the same core concept unless there is a justified technical difference.
- Avoid vague words such as "solutions" when a more precise term exists.
- Do not use `HVP` as an English or Romanian abbreviation unless explicitly explaining the Bulgarian source abbreviation.

## Tone Rules

- professional
- practical
- technically credible
- concise
- non-hyped

Avoid:

- overly sales-heavy wording
- startup or SaaS language
- decorative marketing adjectives
- literal but unnatural translations

## Copy Consistency Rules

### Service naming

Once a service name is chosen for one locale, it should remain unchanged across:

- page titles
- cards
- hero sections
- CTA references
- metadata
- internal links

### Industry naming

Industry labels should remain stable across:

- Industries overview
- future industry detail pages
- SEO titles
- supporting proof sections

## HVP Usage Rule

- In Bulgarian, use `ХВП` together with the expanded form `хранително-вкусова промишленост` when clarity is needed.
- In English, use `Food processing industry`.
- In Romanian, use `Industria alimentară`.
- Do not use `HVP` as a primary visible label in English or Romanian pages.

## SEO Translation Rules

- Titles should preserve search intent, not just literal wording.
- Descriptions should preserve industrial relevance and service clarity.
- Avoid copying Bulgarian word order directly into EN or RO when it sounds unnatural.
- Preserve route-topic alignment between slug, H1 and metadata.

## CTA Translation Rules

Preferred CTA style:

- direct
- practical
- action-oriented

Examples:

- BG: `Изпратете запитване`
- EN: `Send an inquiry`
- RO: `Trimiteți o solicitare`

- BG: `Говорете с инженер`
- EN: `Speak with an engineer`
- RO: `Discutați cu un inginer`

## Review Checklist for Translators

Before approving any EN or RO page, verify:

1. Service terminology is consistent.
2. Industry terminology is consistent.
3. CTA wording is direct and professional.
4. No generic marketing fluff has been introduced.
5. Metadata reflects local search intent.
6. Technical wording is understandable to industrial buyers.
7. `HVP` has not been carried over as an unexplained EN or RO abbreviation.

## Escalation Rule

If a Bulgarian source term has more than one valid EN or RO option, choose one preferred term and log it before scaling translation across the site. Do not let individual pages invent their own variations.
